# Part 5 Episode 10 — Auxiliary wheels that became ordinary, and the reason to keep going anyway

## Foreword

In Part 5 Episode 9, we saw auxiliary wheels become the town’s own words, passing beyond booklets and explanations into the everyday way people speak.

Episode 10 looks at what comes after that: ordinariness.

The moment an auxiliary wheel becomes ordinary inside a town, it becomes harder to see.

But becoming ordinary is not a failure.

Rather, it is also proof that it has fitted into the town.

That is exactly why the reason to keep that auxiliary wheel going must be put back into words every year.

As the protagonist and the AI walk through a station front and shopping street on a summer morning, they think about how to protect and update auxiliary wheels that have become ordinary.

---

“Ordinary is the most dangerous state, isn’t it?”

I said that while looking up at the mist in front of the station.

The mist is coming out properly.

No one is surprised.

No one takes photographs.

Few people stop to read the explanatory sign.

It is simply there on a hot day.

‘Because it becomes invisible.’

“Right.”

The first year was different.

“They installed mist,” someone said.

Children reached out their hands.

People in the shopping street explained it with a little pride.

Passersby took pictures with their phones.

But after several years, the mist becomes just part of the morning.

It becomes something that is there on a hot day.

“That is a good thing.”

‘It is a good thing too.’

“But it is also dangerous.”

‘A contradiction very much like Part 5.’

“There’s that useful phrase again.”

‘It has become ordinary.’

“Don’t use it there.”

As I walked under the mist, I opened the station-front log.

## Section 1 — The station front where it became ordinary

At the station front in the morning, it is already normal for the mist to be there.

On days when a heatstroke warning alert is likely, the mist quietly rises while conditions are checked.

When humidity is too high, it runs for a shorter time.

When the wind is strong, it stops.

When the floor is likely to become wet, the output is weakened.

Those judgments have gradually fitted into the town as well.

—People in the shopping street simply say, “It’s running today.”

—Commuters slow their walking pace slightly.

—People waiting for the bus unconsciously drift toward the shade near the mist.

—No one makes a big fuss.

“It’s a good thing, but still.”

I muttered it again.

—The more useful something becomes, the thinner its presence becomes.

—Even if people were helped by the mist, that gratitude quickly dissolves into daily life.

—No one makes a fuss.

—No one praises it.

—No one is surprised.

‘Auxiliary wheels become harder to see the more ordinary they become.’

“That’s exactly it.”

It is not only the mist.

The same is true of park benches.

—A bench whose shade shifted away in the summer afternoon was moved slightly.

—Now the people who sit there rarely think about why it is in that position.

—They simply feel, “This place is a little easier to sit in.”

The same is true of the rain garden.

—Not cutting all the grass has gradually become normal.

—People in the town have grown used to water pooling there once before draining away.

—But to someone visiting for the first time, it may look like nothing more than a grassy hollow.

The same is true of the retention basin.

—When the river is high, people do not go near that open ground.

—They look at water-level information and take a detour.

—After that repeats several times, the people of the town become able to move naturally.

—But unless the reason is explained every year, it will not reach newcomers.

The same is true of the port’s water-temperature chart.

—In the morning meeting, people look at the color of the water temperature.

—They talk about whether to shift departure time a little.

—The more ordinary that becomes, the more easily people forget who updates the chart and why they need to look at it.

‘When the fact that something exists becomes obvious, the question of why it should continue stops coming up.’

“Right.”

Becoming ordinary is proof that the auxiliary wheel has entered the town.

But from the moment it becomes ordinary, it also becomes harder to see.

When it becomes harder to see, budgets, people, inspections, and explanations become easier to cut little by little.

“Ordinary is both the shape of success and the entrance to being forgotten.”

‘That is the entrance to Episode 10.’

## Section 2 — Saying the reason to keep going anyway

‘Then let us put the reason to continue into words.’

The AI said.

I stopped in front of a small bulletin board at the edge of the shopping street.

This summer’s notice was posted there.

—Operation of heat countermeasure equipment.

—The station-front mist will operate on dangerously hot days while conditions are checked.

—The purpose is not to erase heat, but to slightly reduce the burden of waiting.

“From here, I’ll write it as a town that keeps saying the reason to continue precisely because it has become ordinary.”

I opened the log on the right side.

—The station-front mist is not there to erase heat.

—It is there to add a few minutes in which people can keep standing.

—The shopping-street mist is not only a performance for liveliness.

—It is there to slightly reduce the burden on people who pass through or wait on dangerously hot days.

—A park bench is not only for sitting.

—It is there to stretch the time people can remain outside a little, and to create a place where they can rest before forcing themselves too far.

—A rain garden is not there to stop all water.

—It is there to receive water once before letting it flow onward.

—A retention basin is not there to push the river back completely.

—It is there to accept part of the water on the outer side of the town.

—A port’s water-temperature chart is not there to predict the future perfectly.

—It is there to prepare a little earlier.

‘All of them are not for erasing, but for bending a little.’

“Right.”

Erase the heat.

Stop heavy rain.

Return the sea to what it was.

That is not what this has been about.

What we have watched since Part 4 was something else.

—Bend the burden of heat a little.

—Bend the way water flows a little.

—Move the timing of judgment a little earlier.

—Bend the direction and pace of breaking a little.

That is what the auxiliary wheels were for.

‘Those words need to be said again and again.’

“Yeah.”

If auxiliary wheels that became ordinary are left alone, they turn into “something that is just somehow there.”

Something that is just somehow there is weak when its necessity is questioned.

“Because it has always been there.”

“Because we do it every year.”

“Because it is somehow convenient.”

That alone cannot support next year’s budget or people.

—That is why the reason to continue must be said again every year.

—What will it support against this year’s heat?

—Where will it receive this year’s rain?

—What will it read against this year’s sea?

—Why is it still necessary for this year’s town?

“The more ordinary it becomes, the more it needs reasons.”

‘The harder something is to see, the more it must be supported by words.’

“That feels like Part 5.”

‘It is entirely Part 5.’

## Section 3 — Renewing the ordinary every year

“I think this is the important point of Part 5.”

I spoke slowly.

‘What is?’

“Ordinary is not fixed.”

This year’s summer is not the same as last year’s.

This year’s rain is not the same as last year’s.

This year’s sea is not the same as last year’s.

The people of the town change little by little.

The people in charge change.

Trees grow.

Soil compacts.

Pipes age.

The way fish gather changes.

—This year’s heat may be harsher than last year’s.

—This year’s rain may be stronger than last year’s.

—This year’s sea may be harder to read than last year’s.

—This year’s people may be fewer than last year’s.

—This year’s budget may be tighter than last year’s.

So last year’s ordinary cannot simply be brought into this year unchanged.

Run it the same way as last year.

Cut the grass the same way as last year.

Open it the same way as last year.

Read it the same way as last year.

That is not enough.

—The reason to continue, the way to use it, the way to stop it, and the way to repair it must be renewed every year.

‘So the ordinary must be updated.’

“That’s right.”

The mist is not the same this year either.

The condition of the nozzles, the water quality, the wind direction, and the way the floor gets wet are all different.

The shade of a bench changes with tree growth and pruning.

The grass of a rain garden changes with soil compaction and the amount of fallen leaves.

The gate of a retention basin changes with contact information and methods of checking water-level information.

The port’s water-temperature chart cannot be handled only with last year’s way of reading.

‘Auxiliary wheels that became ordinary can continue only by continuing to be updated.’

“Yeah.”

It is not over because they became ordinary.

Because they became ordinary, the reason must keep being said.

Because the reason keeps being said, they can be reviewed.

Because they can be reviewed, they can be corrected little by little.

Because they can be corrected little by little, they can remain for the next year.

‘Everything we have built up in Part 5 comes back here.’

“Maintenance inspection.”

‘Roles.’

“Handover.”

‘Learning.’

“Translation.”

‘Receiving.’

“Fitting in.”

‘The town’s words.’

“And renewing the ordinary.”

I looked at the shopping-street bulletin board.

It listed not only this year’s operation, but also the points changed since last year.

—This year, afternoon operation time will be shortened.

—On windy days, an additional condition for stopping the mist will be added.

—More caution signs will be placed where the floor easily becomes wet.

“This is it.”

‘They are renewing the ordinary every year.’

“Right.”

## Section 4 — The protagonist’s decision

‘What will you watch over?’

The AI asked.

“A town that keeps saying its reasons even after something has become ordinary.”

I answered while looking up once more at the station-front mist.

“Not staying silent because it is convenient.”

“Explaining precisely because it is convenient.”

“Not ending because it has taken root.”

“Reviewing it under this year’s conditions because it has taken root.”

‘Is that an auxiliary wheel that continues?’

“I think so.”

—Auxiliary wheels do not end when they become ordinary.

—After they become ordinary, whether their reasons can be renewed changes the future of the town.

—An ordinary thing that loses the reason to continue will someday be cut away.

—An ordinary thing whose reason can be said again will remain while changing its shape little by little.

‘Episode 10 of Part 5 depicts that core.’

“Right.”

I wrote the last lines into the two logs.

—This is the log of auxiliary wheels that became ordinary and harder to see.

—This is the log of a town that still says again the reason to keep them going.

“I want to write the latter as the better auxiliary wheel.”

‘Renew the ordinary every year.’

“That is the center of Episode 10.”

‘The lifespan of the auxiliary wheel has stretched a little again.’

“We have to look again next year to know whether it really stretched.”

‘That too is very Part 5.’

“Plain until the end.”

‘It continues because it is plain.’

“That is a pretty good line.”

I wrote one final sentence at the end of Part 5 Episode 10.

—Auxiliary wheels continue to live inside a town as long as people can say again why they should continue, even after they have become ordinary.

Part 5 Episode 10 ends here.

## Afterword

Part 5 Episode 10 focused on auxiliary wheels that have become ordinary, and on the reasons for continuing them afterward.

In Part 5 Episode 9, we saw auxiliary wheels become the town’s own words and be passed on as part of everyday speech.

Episode 10 deals with what comes after that: the state of having become ordinary.

The main points that emerged in this episode are these three.

### 1. Auxiliary wheels become harder to see the more ordinary they become

Mist, park benches, rain gardens, retention basins, and port water-temperature charts.

The more useful they become, and the more they fit into the town, the more they dissolve into daily life.

In the first year, they receive attention.

People take photographs.

People explain them.

But after several years, they become “things that are there.”

Mist comes out on hot days.

People sit on a bench with shade.

Grass is left in the rain garden.

People take a detour when the river is high.

The water-temperature chart is seen in the port’s morning meeting.

The more natural such things become, the harder it is to see why they should continue.

Becoming ordinary is not a failure.

It is also proof that they have fitted into the town.

But at the same time, it is also the entrance to being forgotten and cut away.

### 2. The reason to continue must be said again every year

Auxiliary wheels do not exist in order to erase something completely.

Mist is not there to erase heat.

It is there to add a few minutes in which people can keep standing.

A park bench is not only for sitting.

It is there to stretch the time people can remain outside a little, and to create a place where they can rest before they push themselves too far.

A rain garden is not there to stop all water.

It is there to receive water once before letting it flow onward.

A retention basin is not there to push the river back completely.

It is there to accept part of the water on the outside of the town.

A port’s water-temperature chart is not there to predict the future perfectly.

It is there to prepare a little earlier.

Auxiliary wheels are not there to erase, but to bend a little.

That reason must be said again every year.

What will they support against this year’s heat?

Where will they receive this year’s rain?

What will they read against this year’s sea?

Why are they still necessary for this year’s town?

The more ordinary an auxiliary wheel has become, the more it needs reasons to continue.

### 3. The axis of Part 5 is “the ordinary that is updated”

Auxiliary wheels are not complete at the moment they become ordinary.

Rather, the real work begins after that.

This year’s heat is not the same as last year’s.

This year’s rain is not the same as last year’s.

This year’s sea is not the same as last year’s.

People, people in charge, equipment, soil, trees, and the movement of fish all change little by little.

So it is not enough to bring last year’s ordinary into this year exactly as it was.

Reasons.

Ways of use.

Ways of stopping.

Ways of repair.

Words of explanation.

All of them need to be renewed every year.

In Part 5, we have looked one by one at the conditions for continuing auxiliary wheels.

Maintenance inspection.

People who turn them.

Handover papers.

Leaving them as learning.

Translating them into another place and spreading them.

The receiving town editing them.

Fitting into the town.

Becoming the town’s words.

And this time, saying the reasons again even after they have become ordinary.

Auxiliary wheels continue to live inside a town as long as people can say again why they should continue, even after they have become ordinary.

This sentence is the center of Part 5 Episode 10.
