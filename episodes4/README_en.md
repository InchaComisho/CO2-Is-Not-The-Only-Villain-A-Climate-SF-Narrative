Part 4 begins using the two pens chosen in Part 3: the **reality-line log** and the **what-if-line log**.

It places side by side the world where little changed on a large scale and the world where auxiliary wheels entered institutions, design, and observation a little farther.

```text
The reality-line log records what failed to move and why it stopped.
The what-if-line log records what changed and what new responsibilities and problems appeared.
Neither world is an ideal utopia; each is a world shaped by different human choices.
```

## Part 4 Concept

- **Reality line:** small efforts continue, but remain scattered and do not significantly bend the wider system.
- **What-if line:** Blue Pulse advances to a monitored demonstration off Chile, watershed records begin crossing regional boundaries, and urban heat measures enter shared infrastructure.
- Neither line eliminates heatwaves, floods, marine heatwaves, or failure.
- The comparison asks where small choices, budgets, responsibilities, records, and designs caused the world lines to diverge.

Blue Pulse remains at the concept and review stage in the reality line. Its monitored demonstration off Chile and any later limited operation off Peru belong only to the what-if line.
