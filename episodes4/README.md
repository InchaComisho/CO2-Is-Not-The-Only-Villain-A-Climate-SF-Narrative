```text
現実線では、何が動かず、なぜ止まったのかを書く。
もしも線では、何が変わり、どんな新しい責任や問題が生まれたのかを書く。
どちらも理想郷ではなく、人間が別々の選択をした世界として描く。
```

## Part 4 Concept

Part 4 compares two world lines without treating either as a simple answer:

- **Reality line:** small efforts continue, but they remain scattered and do not significantly alter the wider system.
- **What-if line:** several different decisions are made, allowing Blue Pulse demonstrations, shared watershed records, and urban heat infrastructure to move slightly farther.
- The difference is not complete salvation, but whether damage, burden, observation, and responsibility bend even slightly.

Blue Pulse remains at the concept and review stage in the reality line. Its monitored demonstration off Chile, and any later limited operation off Peru, belong only to the Part 4 what-if line.
