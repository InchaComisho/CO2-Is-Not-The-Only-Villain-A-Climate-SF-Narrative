## Part 7 Concept

Part 7 begins after Part 6 has placed the world’s red graphs beside the auxiliary wheels that remain in towns, watersheds, ports, and river mouths.

The question is no longer only whether regional auxiliary wheels have meaning while the global graphs remain red.

The question becomes where and how those auxiliary wheels cross the boundary from “a town’s ingenuity” into “a world-facing lesson.”

- **Connecting points:** sea and port, river and town, city and watershed, emissions reduction and auxiliary wheels, institutions and everyday life.
- **Barriers:** town borders, budget years, responsible departments, work-life time gaps, and the phrase “there is no precedent.”
- **Translation:** town words into institutional words, local difficulties into budget items, observation data into work decisions.
- **Core movement:** from town ingenuity, to watershed learning, to words that move toward the world.
- **Core question:** when does a small regional auxiliary wheel become part of a world story?
